Phase 2 of 4

Data Systems Remediation Roadmap

1–2 weeks · Fixed price

Objective

Evaluate the existing data infrastructure to identify the limitations preventing it from effectively supporting business operations, reporting, and future technology initiatives.

The assessment focuses on understanding business needs, identifying opportunities for immediate improvement, investigating underlying architectural constraints, and developing a practical roadmap for remediation.

Book a discovery call

Assessment methodology

  1. 01

    Identify Business Needs & Data Limitations

    Engage business and technical stakeholders to understand existing challenges and priorities.

    • Review stalled initiatives, unreliable reporting, and manual workarounds.
    • Understand known data limitations and their impact on business operations.
    • Identify potential quick wins that could deliver value without major architectural changes.

    Output Prioritized list of business needs, known limitations, and potential quick wins.

  2. 02

    Identify Relevant Systems

    Establish a high-level understanding of the systems supporting the selected business processes.

    • Identify relevant applications, databases, integrations, and reporting systems.
    • Document system ownership, purpose, and known dependencies where available.

    Output Inventory of systems involved in the prioritized business problems.

  3. 03

    Trace Critical Data Flows

    Trace selected data flows to investigate the limitations identified during stakeholder discussions.

    • Follow 1–2 high-priority data flows from source systems through transformations to downstream reporting.
    • Investigate inconsistent business logic, duplication, data-modeling issues, unclear data ownership, and data-quality problems.
    • Perform targeted validation or reconciliation checks where appropriate.

    Output Data-flow diagrams and documented findings for 1–2 critical business processes.

  4. 04

    Develop Remediation Roadmap

    Translate assessment findings into a practical, prioritized implementation plan.

    • Evaluate potential remediation approaches, including quick wins and foundational improvements.
    • Prioritize recommendations based on business impact, effort, risk, and dependencies.

    Output Phased implementation roadmap with recommended next steps, dependencies, and high-level effort estimates.

Client commitment

4 hoursEstimated time commitment

A designated point of contact will participate in discussions to identify business needs, key stakeholders, and technical requirements. They will also help direct the assessment to relevant systems, documentation, and technical resources.

This may be the project sponsor or another team member familiar with the organization’s data systems.

Scope

This is a focused diagnostic of selected systems and business processes, rather than a comprehensive enterprise audit. The assessment scope and priorities will be agreed upon during initial discovery.

Not sure where to start? The 30-minute discovery call is where we decide what the assessment should focus on.
